# Repeater data licence

- Source: HearHam's live repeater list, https://hearham.com/repeaters (JSON from https://hearham.com/api/repeaters/v1).
- Licence: none formal. HearHam's repeater page: "Free to use and free to use in your application" ("JSON API is available to download or use in your offline/online application").
  - Listed on our index as: Free to use in applications, per HearHam.com; no formal data licence.
- The entries are contributed by HearHam's users. We credit them:

  > Repeater data from HearHam.com (user-contributed).

- Changes: Ferrite keeps operational repeaters that pass analog FM (mixed ones such as YSF/FM included) with output and input in 136-174, 219-225 and 400-470 MHz, names each by its callsign (16 characters), writes tones in one form, rounds coordinates to 5 decimals and splits the rows into 2-character Maidenhead fields.
- User-contributed data comes with no guarantee: check a repeater's frequency, offset and tone before relying on it.
