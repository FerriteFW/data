# FCC data licence

- Source: US Federal Communications Commission, Universal Licensing System (ULS) weekly public access files, licences:
  - Land Mobile – Private: https://data.fcc.gov/download/pub/uls/complete/l_LMpriv.zip
  - Land Mobile – Commercial: https://data.fcc.gov/download/pub/uls/complete/l_LMcomm.zip
- Download page: https://www.fcc.gov/uls/transactions/daily-weekly
- Status: a US federal government work, not under copyright in the US (17 U.S.C. § 105).
  - The federal catalogue's record for the ULS lists its licence as US government works: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/fcc-universal-licensing-system-uls (licence link https://www.usa.gov/government-works, now https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright).
  - The records are licensing facts filed with the FCC and published by it for public access.
- No attribution is required. We credit it anyway:

  > Source: US Federal Communications Commission, Universal Licensing System public access data (land mobile). Not endorsed by the FCC.

- Changes: Ferrite keeps active licences in 136-174 and 400-470 MHz at locations with coordinates, shortens licensee names to 16 characters, converts coordinates to decimal degrees (5 decimals) and splits the rows into Maidenhead squares.
