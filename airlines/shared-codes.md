# Shared and left-out codes

An IATA code can belong to two current airlines (a "controlled duplicate"). The list keeps the more prominent one.

- `3S`: AeroLogic (cargo, Germany) kept; Air Antilles and Air Guyane left out.
- `6R`: Aerounion (cargo, Mexico) kept; Alrosa (Russia) left out.
- `RC`: Atlantic Airways (Faroe Islands) kept; Ryukyu Air Commuter (Japan) left out.
- `MY`: Mas Air (cargo, Mexico) kept; MASwings (Malaysia) left out.
- `ZP`: Paranair and Silk Way Airlines both left out (not sure which holds it now).
- `LH`: Lufthansa kept; Lufthansa Cargo flies under it too.

Carriers with no IATA code of their own, which fly under another's:

- Purolator: its flights are Cargojet's (`W8`).
- QantasLink, Air Canada Express, Delta Connection, United Express and the like: their flights carry the mainline code or the operator's own (Jazz `QK`, SkyWest `OO`, ...).
